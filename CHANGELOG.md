# Changelog

## 0.1.0

The first public release.

- Battery for each bud and the case, time left estimates, a 24 hour chart, low battery and charged notifications, and a card when the case opens
- Noise cancellation, transparency, adaptive and off, with the adaptive level and the modes the stem cycles through
- Conversation awareness, personalized volume, noise cancellation with one AirPod, volume swipe, press speed and hold duration, case speaker volume, sleep detection and hearing assistance
- Press shortcuts and head gestures that run actions on the computer (experimental)
- Pause when a bud comes out and resume when it goes back in, for any MPRIS player
- A high quality AirPods mic for calls, decoded from AAC-ELD into a separate input
- Spatial audio with head tracking and a custom EQ (experimental)
- Several pairs of headphones, each with its own keys and settings
- Battery reported to BlueZ for GNOME, KDE and UPower; tray icon, Waybar and Caelestia modules, global shortcuts and `pinnactl`
- English and Turkish
- AUR package and Flatpak manifest
